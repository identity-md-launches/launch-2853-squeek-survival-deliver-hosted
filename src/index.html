A little wheel. A big rival.

Make a call on a virtual race. Keep three lives alive and chip away at your device’s weekly boss.

  1. Read Robo’s call. Pick Over or Under its distance, or pick a different zone.
  2. Lock in within 12 seconds. Add one booster if you like. No pick means a free skip.
  3. Watch the wheel. A future Sepolia block seeds the race. Offline? A cryptographic local seed takes over within 45 seconds.
  4. Win, hit, repeat. Hardware RPM affects damage, never the virtual finish. Save the art your races create.
Lives, boosters & scoring

Over / Under equality is a push. In Zone mode, only you right wins; only Robo right loses; both wrong draws; both right ties. Neutral results keep your streak and lives.

Shield prevents life loss, but a loss still resets your streak. Double down doubles win damage and life loss. Insight shows historical spread and Robo’s zone accuracy. Boosters are spent at lock, including neutral results. Shield rests for two participated rounds when live and still, otherwise three. Other boosters rest for three. Skips do not count.

Damage = round(D × (1 + 0.1 × min(win streak, 10)) × frozen RPM multiplier × double down). Moving hardware adds up to 25%; 60 RPM gives ×1.125. Score = round((applied damage + 100 × best streak) × highest raid loot multiplier encountered).

Raid health resets Monday 00:00 UTC using the ISO week year. A round belongs to the week it started. Loot at 75%, 50%, 25%, and zero HP rises to ×1.1, ×1.25, ×1.5, and ×2. A defeated boss takes no further damage.

Keyboard: 1 / 2 pick Over / Under, 3 / 4 / 5 pick a zone, Enter locks, P pauses. All controls also work with Tab and Space. Pause stops the local timer and animations. Hidden tabs pause requests, timers, and audio.

Local records and Robo’s learning stay in this browser. No account, rewards, deposits, or game data on-chain.
